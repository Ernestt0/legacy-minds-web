Supplier Verification and Certification

Don't send money to strangers. We validate your suppliers' legal existence, real production capacity and reputation before you sign any contract.

Book a Meeting

In a market with millions of middlemen and "front" companies, choosing the wrong supplier can cost you all of your investment capital. At Legacy Minds we act as your eyes on the ground. We carry out a forensic and on-site audit to separate real manufacturers from scams, making sure your business partner has the infrastructure and certifications needed to deliver on its promises.

Our 4 Sourcing Pillars

Operational Capacity Inspection

We visit the plant to confirm it isn't just a middleman (trader). We assess machinery, assembly lines, headcount and real daily/monthly production capacity.

Legal and Document Validation

We rule out shell companies. We check business licenses, government registrations and legal records in official Chinese databases to make sure the company exists and operates legally.

Certifications and Compliance

We check the authenticity of key certifications (ISO, CE, RoHS, FCC) and export permits, making sure the supplier meets the international standards your market requires.

Visual Evidence and Report

Without traveling, you'll see what we see. We deliver a full report with photos and videos of the facilities, warehouses and offices, along with a final verdict (Approved / Not Approved).

Why is this service essential?

  • Risk EliminationReduce to zero the chance of falling for fraud, scams or corporate identity theft.
  • Negotiating PowerKnowing the factory's real situation (and its weaknesses), you negotiate prices and lead times from a position of strength.
  • Middleman FilterAvoid paying markups to "traders" posing as factories; go straight to the source.
  • Investment SecurityDecide whether to send the deposit based on verified data, not on promises made in a chat.
