Owning a company in Hong Kong is a big competitive advantage, but it comes with strict obligations. At Legacy Minds we handle the complex paperwork, from annual accounting to license renewals. Our goal is simple: keep your company compliant with local regulations so you can enjoy the tax and financial benefits without the risk of freezes or penalties.

Corporate Support

Service Pillars

Compliance and Company Secretary

We keep your company in "Good Standing". We handle the Annual Return, director updates and the Significant Controllers Register with the Companies Registry to avoid government fines.

Accounting and CPA Audit

Your accounts must be audited every year. We prepare your books under HKFRS standards and coordinate the audit with certified public accountants (CPA) so your financial statements are filed on time.

Tax Strategy (Profits Tax)

Don't overpay. We handle your corporate tax return and process the offshore income exemption (where applicable), legally reducing your tax burden to 0% on profits earned outside HK.

Banking Maintenance

Banks in Asia close accounts that don't file audits. We make sure your documentation is flawless to pass the annual reviews (KYC) of traditional banks and Fintech platforms.

Strategic Benefits

  • Secure Bank AccountsWith your compliance up to date, you remove the number-one risk in HK: a sudden freeze of your funds due to missing documentation.
  • Real Tax SavingsUse HK's territorial system correctly, paying only what is fair and legal.
  • Contract SupportWe draft and review commercial contracts with suppliers and clients to protect your interests in international jurisdictions
  • Global CredibilityAn audited, transparent company has easier access to credit, payment gateways and better suppliers.
